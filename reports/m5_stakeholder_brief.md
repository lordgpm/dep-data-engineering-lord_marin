# Where to focus connection-seeking programs in Metro Manila

## Context

This brief looks at public r4r posts from four Philippine subreddits between August 2025 and August 2026. The question is where connection-seeking posts are concentrated, when they happen, and what kind of demand is most common. The window covers 197,082 posts; about 36% name a Metro Manila city.

The four categories are subreddits, not a text classifier: phr4friends (platonic), phr4dating (romantic), dirtyphpr4r (sexual), and phr4r_2 (general/mixed). I compare them separately so the mix of subreddit volumes does not distort the city rates.

## Main finding

Makati has the highest volume per resident by a wide margin: about 2,894 posts per 100,000 residents, compared with 746 in Manila and 593 in Quezon City. It also has the highest platonic (464) and sexual (400) posts per 100,000. Its late-night share is lower (19.6%), and sexual posts are only 13.8% of its total, so it is a large general market rather than a sexual-skewed one.

Pasay has the highest sexual share (36.1%) and a late-night share of 24.9%, but its sexual rate is 155 per 100,000, below Makati's 400. So Pasay is the most sexual-skewed city, not the biggest sexual market by population. Muntinlupa and Taguig have the highest platonic shares (18.5% and 16.9%), while Makati still has the highest platonic posts per 100,000.

Age matters too. Under-30 posts are mostly platonic (60% for 18-24, 65% for 25-29), while 35+ posts are mostly sexual (57%). Late-night share stays between 26% and 28% across the 12 full months, so timing is stable enough to plan around.

## What I would do

1. **Pilot a general social space in Makati.** It has the most connection-seeking posts per resident and the largest absolute audience in every category. Late-night programming is less important there than in other cities.
2. **Use Pasay for sexual-skewed outreach.** It has the highest sexual share and a late-night share similar to Manila. This is the better setting if the goal is to reach an audience already focused on sexual posts.
3. **Try friendship-oriented programming in Muntinlupa and Taguig.** They have the highest platonic shares among the larger cities.

If one city has to be chosen first, Makati is the safest test for overall demand. Pasay is the clearer test for a sexual-skewed audience.

## Caveats

- Only about 36% of posts name a city. Posts without a city are more late-night (29.4% vs 22.9%) and more platonic (34.3% vs 13.8%), so the city results likely understate platonic and late-night demand.
- City matching keeps the first city when a post lists more than one.
- The categories are subreddits, not post-level intent labels. The general r4r subreddit is a mixed bucket.
- Reddit posters are not a representative sample of city residents.
- These are descriptive rates, not evidence that a place causes a behavior.
- Small cities like San Juan can have high per-capita rates from a few hundred posts.
