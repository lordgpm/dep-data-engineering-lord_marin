# Where to focus connection-seeking programs in Metro Manila

## Context

This brief looks at public r4r posts from four Philippine subreddits between August 2025 and August 2026. The question is where connection-seeking posts are concentrated, when they happen, and what kind of intent is most common. The window covers 197,082 posts; about 36% name a Metro Manila city.

## Main finding

Demand is not spread evenly. Makati has the highest volume per resident by a wide margin: about 2,894 posts per 100,000 residents, compared with 746 in Manila and 593 in Quezon City. But the type of demand differs. Makati's posts are more friendship-oriented (16.0% platonic, 13.8% sexual), while Pasay's are heavily sexual (36.1%) and also late-night (24.9% of posts between 10 PM and 2 AM). Muntinlupa and Taguig have the highest platonic shares (18.5% and 16.9%).

Age matters too. Under-30 posts are mostly platonic (60% for 18-24, 65% for 25-29), while 35+ posts are mostly sexual (57%). Late-night share stays between 26% and 28% across the 12 full months, so timing is stable enough to plan around.

## What I would do

1. **Pilot a general social space in Makati.** It has the most connection-seeking posts per resident and a relatively high platonic share. Late-night programming is less important there than in other cities.
2. **Focus sexual-health outreach in Pasay.** It has the highest sexual share and a late-night share similar to Manila. That combination points to evening or late-night outreach.
3. **Try friendship-oriented programming in Muntinlupa and Taguig.** They have the highest platonic shares among the larger cities, so the demand is more clearly friendship-led.

If one city has to be chosen first, Makati is the safest test for a general social space. Pasay is the clearer target for sexual-health work.

## Caveats

- Only about 36% of posts name a city, and the matcher keeps the first city when a post lists more than one.
- Reddit posters are not a representative sample of city residents.
- These are descriptive rates, not evidence that a place causes a behavior.
- Small cities like San Juan can have high per-capita rates from a few hundred posts.
